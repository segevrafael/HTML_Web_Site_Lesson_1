Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer private cooking classes?

Yes! We offer private cooking classes for groups of 6 or more. These can be customized to focus on specific dishes or techniques you're interested in learning. Please contact us for pricing and availability.

Where can I find ingredients for Middle Eastern cooking?

Many regular supermarkets now carry basic Middle Eastern ingredients. For more specialized items, we recommend checking Middle Eastern or Mediterranean grocery stores, or ordering online. We're happy to recommend specific sources for hard-to-find ingredients.

Can I suggest a recipe you should feature?

Absolutely! We love hearing from our community. Use the contact form to send us your recipe suggestions, and we'll consider adding them to our collection.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions in your courses?

Yes, we can accommodate most dietary restrictions with advance notice. Many Middle Eastern dishes are naturally vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free. Please let us know about any allergies or restrictions when you register for a course.

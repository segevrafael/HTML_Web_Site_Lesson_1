Mezze Basics Workshop

2 hours
$45 per person
Max 12 participants
Beginner level

About This Course

Mezze is the heart of Middle Eastern hospitality, featuring an array of small dishes designed to be shared. In this hands-on workshop, you'll learn to prepare four essential mezze dishes that are staples in Levantine cuisine.

Perfect for beginners, this course will teach you the fundamental techniques for creating authentic flavors, working with key ingredients like tahini, chickpeas, and fresh herbs. By the end of the session, you'll have mastered the basics of creating a beautiful mezze spread to impress friends and family.

What You'll Learn

  • Hummus Preparation

    Learn the secrets to creating silky-smooth hummus with perfect flavor balance.

  • Baba Ganoush Techniques

    Master the art of roasting eggplant to achieve that distinctive smoky flavor.

  • Tabbouleh Salad

    Discover the proper ratios and chopping techniques for this herb-forward salad.

  • Muhammara (Red Pepper Dip)

    Create this flavorful walnut and roasted pepper dip with authentic Aleppo pepper.

  • Mezze Platter Assembly

    Learn how to arrange and present your mezze dishes with traditional accompaniments.

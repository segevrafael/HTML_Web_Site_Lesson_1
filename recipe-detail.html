Creamy Homemade Hummus

15 mins
4 servings
Easy

This creamy homemade hummus is a Middle Eastern classic that's incredibly easy to make. With just a few simple ingredients, you can create this silky smooth dip that's perfect for snacking or as part of a mezze platter. The key to achieving that restaurant-quality texture is properly cooking the chickpeas and processing them while they're still warm.

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed (reserve liquid)
  • 1/4 cup tahini paste
  • 3 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil, plus more for serving
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt, or to taste
  • 2-3 tablespoons reserved chickpea liquid or water
  • For garnish: paprika, chopped parsley, olive oil

Instructions

  1. Add the tahini and lemon juice to a food processor and process for 1 minute. Scrape the sides of the bowl and process for another 30 seconds. This will make the tahini smooth and creamy.

  2. Add the olive oil, minced garlic, cumin, and salt to the food processor. Process for 30 seconds, scrape the sides of the bowl, then process for another 30 seconds.

  3. Add half of the chickpeas to the food processor and process for 1 minute. Scrape sides of the bowl, add the remaining chickpeas, and process for 1-2 minutes until smooth.

  4. If the hummus is too thick, slowly add the reserved chickpea liquid or water, 1 tablespoon at a time, while the processor is running until you reach your desired consistency.

  5. Taste and adjust seasonings as needed, adding more salt or lemon juice if desired.

  6. Transfer the hummus to a serving bowl. Create a shallow well in the center with the back of a spoon, drizzle with olive oil, and sprinkle with paprika and chopped parsley.

  7. Serve with warm pita bread, fresh vegetables, or as part of a mezze platter. Store any leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week.

Tips for Perfect Hummus

  • For extra smooth hummus, remove the skins from the chickpeas before processing.
  • If using dried chickpeas, soak them overnight and cook until very tender.
  • High-quality tahini makes a big difference in flavor - look for a pure, smooth brand.
  • Hummus will thicken slightly when refrigerated, so make it slightly thinner than your desired final consistency.
